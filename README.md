## Fastest way to publish with Cloudflare Pages

1. Sign in to Cloudflare.
2. Open **Workers & Pages**.
3. Choose **Create application** > **Pages** > **Upload assets** (wording can vary slightly).
4. Upload the contents of this website folder, or upload the provided ZIP if Cloudflare offers ZIP upload.
5. After the Pages site deploys, open its **Custom domains** section and add `corelandservicesva.com`.
6. Because your domain is already registered at Cloudflare, Cloudflare should guide you through the DNS connection.
7. Also add `www.corelandservicesva.com` and redirect it to the main domain if offered.

## Quote form

The form is configured to use FormSubmit and send requests to Corelandservices1@gmail.com. The first live form submission generally triggers a one-time confirmation email. Open that email and approve it before relying on the form. Test the form after publishing.

For a more robust long-term setup, replace FormSubmit later with a Cloudflare Pages Function, a CRM form, or another form service.
