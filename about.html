About Core Land Services

Built on service. Driven by quality.

Veteran Owned & Operated

Core Land Services is based in King George, Virginia and was built around a straightforward idea: show up, communicate clearly, respect the property, and do solid work.

We provide practical outdoor property services including stump grinding, small tree removal, brush and debris hauling, mowing, landscaping, and cleanup.

Our goal: make it easier for local property owners to handle the jobs that are too large, time-consuming, or equipment-heavy to tackle alone.

Professional Equipment

Our equipment lineup includes a Vermeer stump grinder and a John Deere zero-turn mower. As the business grows, the website can be updated with exact model information and real project photography.
